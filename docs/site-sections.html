FAQ

Answers to the questions people ask before they pay.

list

Questions about credits.

How are credits counted?
A scrape costs 1 credit. A crawl costs 1 credit per page, and /extract adds 4 credits per page for structured output.
Do unused credits roll over?
No. Credits reset on the first of each month, and your spend cap stays where you set it.
Can I cap what a key spends?
Yes. Set a monthly cap per team or per key; requests stop at the cap until credits reset.
Do you store the pages you read?
Logs keep request metadata for 30 days. With zero data retention on, Ember stores no page content at all.
What happens when a page fails?
Ember retries twice with a new browser. A page that still fails costs nothing and shows in your logs with its reason.

No resources match

Try another kind or topic, or fewer words in the search.

  • For the docs' versioned release notes use fd-doc-changelog; a site's news page is this section as a listing.
  • An entry carries data-kind and data-tags; the tools bar holds existing controls, each with data-filter (kind, q, or a tag group such as topic). data-page-size pages the matches with an empty fd-pagination or a button[data-more]; data-address keeps the filters in the address, so a filtered library can be shared and Back works.
  • A resource's cover is drawn in type: fd-site-posts-cover (optionally data-ground="inverse|raised") holds its kind, fd-site-posts-cover-title and the texture. fd-site-posts-kind and fd-site-posts-meta carry the kind and the format line ("Report · PDF · 24 pages").
  • Grid media is the signature texture with a seed per post; a post with a real image puts it in the same frame.

Article

The reading page of a post, a report or a research paper: the fifteenth section, and the only one built for long text. plain is one reading column, aside adds a sticky rail for contents and key facts, cover spans the head over a wide frame like a report's front page.

How we cut the median scrape to 842 ms

Three changes took a scrape from 1,310 ms to 842 ms at the median: warm browsers, a smaller snapshot and one fewer hop.

  • JWJonas WeberStaff engineer, crawl infrastructure
Nov 4, 20268 min read

A scrape is one request to /scrape: Ember opens the page in a browser, waits for it to settle, and returns Markdown, HTML or JSON. For an agent that reads ten pages before it answers, the wait for each page is most of the wait for the answer.1

Browsers that wait warm

Each region keeps a pool of browsers started and idle, sized from the last hour of traffic. Starting a browser fell from 340 ms to 35 ms.

Fetch and render fell from 610 ms to 520 ms once the proxy router moved into each region.

Notes

  1. The median over every /scrape call in the week, failed calls left out.

fd-site-article-footRuled rows: fd-site-notes (each li#note-n with an fd-site-notes-back link; in the text sup > a.fd-site-notes-ref#ref-n with aria-describedby on the notes heading), fd-site-refs (hanging indent), "Cite this" in an fd-copy-field, fd-tag links, Copy link (button[data-fd-copy-link]), the authors, a sign-up.
  • A figure is an fd-doc-frame whose caption ends in a small Source line; a chart is an fd-chart in an fd-site-shot, with its data table hidden for screen readers or shown in an fd-disclosure.
  • Following a note reference moves focus to the note, and its back link returns focus to the reference: the reader keeps their place. Nothing follows the scroll: no reading progress bar, no scroll effects.
  • Key facts are an fd-kv (data-layout="split" data-divided), findings an fd-site-features list: the article adds no part for either.

Questions about credits.

Still unsure? Write to billing@ember.dev and a person answers within a day.

How are credits counted?
A scrape costs 1 credit. A crawl costs 1 credit per page, and /extract adds 4 credits per page for structured output.
Do unused credits roll over?
No. Credits reset on the first of each month, and your spend cap stays where you set it.
Can I cap what a key spends?
Yes. Set a monthly cap per team or per key; requests stop at the cap until credits reset.
Do you store the pages you read?
Logs keep request metadata for 30 days. With zero data retention on, Ember stores no page content at all.
What happens when a page fails?
Ember retries twice with a new browser. A page that still fails costs nothing and shows in your logs with its reason.
Can I crawl pages behind a login?
Yes. Pass cookies or headers with the request; they are used for that crawl and never stored.
