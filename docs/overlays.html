Components

Overlays

Menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs, sheets, toasts and the command palette. Everything that floats is a white surface with its own 1px ring, scales in from its anchor and leaves faster than it came. Each one has a live trigger and an inline rendering below it.

Menus

Use a menu for a short list of actions or a choice that belongs to one trigger: switching teams, acting on a row, choosing columns. Arrow keys move, typing jumps to an item, Escape returns focus to the trigger.

  • Items are 36px tall with a 16px icon and a 14px label. Highlight follows focus, and the pointer moves focus, so mouse and keyboard never show two highlighted rows.
  • A switcher marks the current item with an accent wash and a dot (data-variant="switcher" or aria-current). A single choice elsewhere gets accent text and a trailing check; multiple choice gets a leading box.
  • Sub-menus (data-fd-submenu) open to the side on hover or → and close with ←. Checkbox items keep the menu open; add data-keep-open to do the same for others.
  • Danger items sit last, below a separator, and open a confirmation rather than acting at once.

One menu for every row

When rows share their actions but not every action fits every row, keep one menu and let each row say which items apply. A row names its state in data-fd-flags; an item says when it shows. Nothing is cloned per row.

  • data-fd-when="a b" shows an item when the flags hold either word; data-fd-unless="a b" hides it when they hold any. Flags come from the opener and from the nearest ancestor with data-fd-flags, read each time the menu opens. A hidden item is hidden, so arrow keys, typing and Home and End skip it, and a separator with nothing on one side goes with it. data-fd-else="disable" dims an item instead of hiding it.
  • A menu with nothing to show (no item that is visible and not dimmed) does not open: the opener gets aria-disabled="true" (the last row), and stays in the tab order so it can say why through a tooltip. fd:select carries row, the row the menu was opened from. Call init(row) after changing a row's flags to refresh its opener.

Use a popover when the content is richer than a list: a summary, a small form, a picker. It closes on outside click, Escape, or when focus leaves it. Add data-arrow when the anchor is small and the link between them should be explicit.

Date range

Presets cover what people ask for most; the month grid handles the rest. Click a start and an end (the range previews as you hover), then Apply. Today carries an accent dot, future days are disabled for logs.

  • Weeks start on Monday. Arrow keys move by day and week, PgUp PgDn by month; while a start is picked, moving the focus previews the range.
  • Apply writes the preset name or the dates into [data-fd-daterange-label] inside the trigger and fires fd:change with ISO dates. Cancel restores the last applied range.

Period with comparison

The date range with a second question: compared with what. Reports and charts show the period and, beside it, the same figures for an earlier one. The trigger names both.

Compared with
Compared with
  • The comparison is a mode, not a second calendar: previous is the period of the same length just before the chosen one, year is the same dates a year earlier, none turns it off. The dates it works out are shown in the band, and again in the trigger, so nobody has to guess what a figure is compared with.
  • Apply fires fd:change with { start, end, preset, compare } in detail.value, where compare is null or { mode, start, end } (ISO dates). It also writes the range into [data-fd-daterange-label] and the comparison into [data-fd-daterange-compare-label] in the trigger, which is empty, and hidden, when there is none. Cancel restores both.
  • Presets beyond the date range's: lastmonth, quarter and year (to date). Show the ones your data answers; choosing a day in the calendar makes it Custom.

Tooltips

Tooltips name icon-only controls and reveal shortcuts. They wait 350ms the first time, then follow the pointer from one control to the next with no delay, and disappear the moment you leave, press or scroll. Keyboard focus shows them at once.

  • Inverse surface, 8px radius, 12px/450 text, 6px by 8px padding. A tooltip never takes the pointer, so it can sit over the next control without getting in the way.
  • One tooltip element serves the whole page and is linked to its trigger with aria-describedby while visible. Icon-only triggers without a name get the tooltip text as their aria-label.
  • Don’t put links or actions in a tooltip. Use a hovercard or a popover.

Hovercards

A hovercard previews the thing a link points to, a person or a provider, without leaving the page. It opens after half a second, stays open while the pointer travels into it, and closes a quarter second after leaving.

Dialogs

Use a dialog when the task must finish or be dismissed before the page makes sense again: confirming, creating a key, choosing providers. The page behind is washed out and blurred. Below 640px every dialog becomes a bottom sheet you can drag down.

Closing always ends in one native close event, and fd:close follows it exactly once, whether the dialog closed from a button, Escape, a backdrop click, closeDialog() or dialog.close() in your own script (a dialog you opened yourself with showModal() reports it too). A toast made while a dialog is closing lands on the page, not inside the closing dialog.

Sheets

A sheet holds the detail of something picked from a list, a log entry or a run, without losing the list. It slides in from the right at 420px (560px for wide content), from the left for navigation and filters, and from the bottom on phones.

Focus never opens on a footer action, so a second Enter cannot confirm a decision. It goes to [autofocus] if the sheet or dialog has one, else to the first form field in the body, else to the title. Open Review decision with the keyboard: focus starts on the title, and the footer waits until you have read the body.

API

Everything works from markup. fire/js/overlays.js also exports these, for flows that start in code.

NameWhat it does
data-fd-menu="id"Opens the menu with that id. Sets aria-haspopup, aria-expanded and aria-controls. ↓ ↑ on the trigger open it at the first or last item.
data-fd-popover="id"Opens a popover anchored to the trigger. data-placement on the trigger or the surface: bottom-start (default), top, right-end…; flips when it doesn’t fit.
data-fd-tooltip="text"Tooltip on any element, with optional data-placement (default top) and data-shortcut="⌘ K".
data-fd-hovercard="id"Hover panel with data-open-delay (500) and data-close-delay (250).
data-fd-dialog-open="id"Opens a dialog, sheet or command palette. data-fd-dialog-close closes the one it is in, with its value as the return value.
data-fd-toast='{…}'Shows a toast from JSON options when clicked.
data-static-demoRenders any surface inline and non-floating, for documentation and reviews.
openMenu(id, opts){ trigger, at: { x, y }, focus: 'first' | 'last' | 'menu' | 'none', placement }. at opens it at a point, for context menus. closeMenu(id).
openPopover(id, opts){ trigger, placement, focus }. closePopover(id).
openDialog(id, opts){ trigger } is where focus returns. closeDialog(id, value); without an id closes the top dialog.
toast(opts)Returns an id. toast.promise, toast.update, toast.dismiss, and shortcuts toast.success / error / warning / info(title, opts).
commandopen(), close(), toggle(), register(items), and records({ group, search }) for found records (see Found records).
Eventsfd:open and fd:close on every surface (dialogs pass detail.value), fd:select from menu and command items, fd:change from checkbox and radio items, dialog steps, filters, date ranges and notifications, fd:confirm from type-to-confirm.

Rules

Do

Pick the lightest overlay that does the job: a tooltip names, a menu acts, a popover shows, a sheet details, a dialog decides.

Don't

Open a dialog from a dialog, put a form in a toast, or hide the only way to do something behind hover.
