      Surfaces

      Every part's card lists the surfaces it suits: app, website, phone or email. A page says which one it is, and the tools keep out what does not belong.

      HTML
      <html lang="en" data-fd-surface="website">
      • data-fd-surface goes on <html> or on any element, and the nearest one decides. A phone page accepts app parts too.
      • The markup lint reports surface-mismatch, a warning, for a part whose card does not list the page's surface. --surface <name> sets it for files that declare none, and --no-surface turns the rule off. See The checks.
      • npx fire find "<job>" --surface phone lists only parts for that surface, and says when a clearly better match belongs to another one. See Find the part by its job.
      • The email surface is for renderEmail(), not for fd- classes: only the email, initial and theme modules list it. See Email.
