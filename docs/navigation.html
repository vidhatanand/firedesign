Components

Navigation

Tabs, segmented controls, category strips and the small wayfinding pieces between them. Selection moves; it never jumps. A thin accent line or a raised white thumb slides to where you clicked.

Tabs

Use underline tabs to switch between views of the same object, like the output formats of a run. They sit on a hairline; the accent underline slides between tabs with a slight spring.

overflow

  • Buttons with role="tab" and aria-controls get the full tabs pattern: arrow keys, Home and End move and select, panels show and hide. Links with aria-current="page" are route tabs.
  • When tabs overflow, the strip scrolls sideways, fades the clipped edge and keeps the selected tab in view.
  • The tablist fires fd:change with { value }: the tab's data-value, else its panel id.

Segmented control

Use a segmented control for two to five mutually exclusive options that change what you see right here: audience, state filter, theme. One white thumb slides between the options.

data-block

  • A track narrower than its options keeps each option at its own width and scrolls sideways (on a phone, swipe it). The chosen option is scrolled into view on load and whenever the selection moves, by click or by arrow key. With data-block the options share the width equally instead, so keep those short.
  • It is a radio group: Tab reaches the selected option, arrow keys move the selection, and the group fires fd:change with the item's data-value.
  • Groups of toggle buttons with aria-pressed, or links with aria-current, get the same sliding thumb.
  • More than five options, or options that navigate to another page, belong in tabs.

Chip nav

A category strip for browsing a catalog. Lighter than tabs: no underline, just ink. It scrolls sideways on narrow screens and fades at the clipped edge.

  • Links mark the current category with aria-current="page"; button chips are single-select with aria-pressed and fire fd:change.

  • Live states are written by your code on each step: data-state="running|failed|waiting|skipped", beside done. Mark the step the work is at with aria-current="step", and leave data-current off: it is for wizards, where every earlier step is done.

Rules

Do

Pick by what changes: tabs switch views of one thing, a segmented control filters or toggles a mode, a chip nav browses categories, a subnav moves between settings pages.

Don't

Fill tabs or segments with accent colour. Selection is shown by ink, a 2px line or a white thumb; the accent line is the only colour.
