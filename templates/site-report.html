The pricing page index, 2026

For 18 months our monitors read 4,120 public pricing pages every 6 hours. Six in ten changed. This is what changed, how often, and when.

Key findings

  1. 01

    61% of pricing pages changed

    2,513 of 4,120 pages changed at least once in 18 months; the median page that changed did so 3 times.

  2. 02

    38% of changes raised a price

    3,741 of 9,846 changes were increases. Only 886, or 9%, were cuts.

  3. 03

    January is repricing month

    802 changes landed in January 2026, 46% more than the monthly median of 551.

  4. 04

    Plans change as often as prices

    35% of changes renamed a plan, moved a limit or a feature between plans, with no price changed.

1. How often pricing pages change

We counted a change when the plans, prices, limits or features on a page differed from the last check, after removing dates, banners and test variants. Across 4,120 pages and 18 months that came to 9,846 changes, or about 547 a month.

Changes are steady through the year with one exception. In January 2026 the count rose to 802 as companies started their fiscal year with new prices.

2. What changes

Most changes are not a new number. More than half rename a plan, move a limit or rewrite what a plan includes; a price increase is the single largest kind.

The full report goes on to seat prices, usage pricing and the pages that changed most, with a chapter on how to watch a competitor’s pricing yourself.
