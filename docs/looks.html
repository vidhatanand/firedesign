Looks

Eight complete theme records, each a product's character from palette and type down to corners, surfaces and texture, drawn far apart so one of them is a good place to start. Every look passes the same contrast rules as a palette, under its own surfaces and controls, and the gallery check renders three real screens under each one. Each look also sets how a website's pictures are treated: natural, mono, duotone or wash (the imagery dial).

The eight looks

Each tile is drawn in its own look. Pick the one nearest your product, apply it, then tune it in Tweaks: any change turns the panel's Looks readout to Custom, and Copy theme gives you the record to commit.

Applying a look

In Tweaks

The Looks row at the top of the panel applies a whole record in one click and shows which look is active. Copy theme and Copy CSS then carry it.

In code

Each record is in contract/looks/named. Pass it to themeToCss(), or import LOOKS from fire/js/looks.js and call applyLook(name) from fire/js/tweaks.js.

As a preview

Add ?look=steady to any page that loads fire/boot.js. The look is on the page before its first paint and nothing is saved. Only the eight names work.
