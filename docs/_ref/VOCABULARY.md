Use these exact names. If you own a family, implement every name listed under it (you may add more).
If you need another family's component in a demo, use its name from this list; it will exist.

## Added in 0.8

These entries complement the family lines above. `CLASSES.md`, beside this file, is the complete list of classes and values, generated from the catalogue (`contract/catalog.json`).

**Shell**

`fd-tabbar`, the phone tab bar: a direct child of `fd-app` holding three to five items, shown only while the shell is narrower than 640px. Parts `fd-tabbar-item` (an `a`, or a `button` with `data-fd-mobile-nav` and `aria-controls` named "More", which opens the menu sheet and carries `aria-expanded`; `aria-current="page"`; `data-dot` is the new mark and needs an `fd-sr-only` line saying what is new), `fd-tabbar-label`, `fd-tabbar-badge` (a count). `--fd-tabbar-space` is the room the bar takes, set on the page while it shows; parts at the bottom of the screen add it.

Parts `fd-toolbar-search`, `fd-toolbar-end`, `fd-toolbar-sep`. A search, an end group and filters stay on one line while they fit; then the search and the end group share the first line and the filters move together to the second (the width at which this happens depends on one, two, three or four-or-more filters: 780, 940, 1110 and 1280px). `fd-screen-foot`, the legal row of a centred screen (`fd-screen`): a copyright and links such as Terms, Privacy and language.

`fd-brand`, the product's mark and name: `fd-brand-mark`, `fd-brand-name`, or `fd-brand-logo` for a wide image in place of both. Parts `fd-site-posts-list`, `fd-site-posts-item`, `fd-site-posts-date`, `fd-site-posts-title`, `fd-site-posts-summary`, `fd-site-posts-media`, `fd-site-posts-kind` (a label), `fd-site-posts-meta` (authors, date, reading time), `fd-site-posts-cover` (a cover drawn in type, in the media frame; `data-ground="inverse|raised"`, with `fd-site-posts-cover-title`), `fd-site-posts-tools` (a chip nav or tabs, a select, a search), `fd-site-posts-count` (`role="status"`, `data-noun` is the plural noun). An entry carries `data-kind` and `data-tags` (space separated). A control marked `data-filter="kind|q|<topic>"` anywhere in the section filters the entries (`all` or empty is no filter). `data-page-size="12"` on the section pages the matches: an empty `fd-pagination` is filled, or a `button[data-more]` shows the next page. `data-address` keeps the filters and the page in the address. `[data-clear]` inside the section's `fd-empty` clears them. Event `fd:change` `{ value: { kind, q, …, page }, shown, total }`; function `filterPosts(section, filters, { page, push })`; labels `posts.*`. Tokens `--fd-site-cover-*`, `--fd-site-thumb-*`.

`fd-site-cta` sign-up: the one sign-up form is an `input[type=email]` in `form.fd-site-cta-form` (`fd-site-cta` with `data-layout="split"`, `fd-site-footer-signup` in the footer, and an article's foot). Parts `fd-site-cta-consent`, `fd-site-cta-done`. The form's `data-state` is `invalid|busy|sent|subscribed|failed` (idle has none); `data-error-failed` on the input is the text for `failed`; `.fd-site-cta-done[data-state="sent|subscribed"]` is the line that replaces the field. Event `fd:submit` `{ value }` is cancelable; `fd:change` `{ value: state }`; function `setSignup(form, state)`; labels `signup.*`.

`fd-site-media`, a picture: a `figure` around one `img` with `alt`, `width` and `height`, and an optional `figcaption`. `data-ratio="wide|photo|square|portrait|tall"` crops it, `data-position="start|center|end"` keeps a side, `data-frame` gives it the media frame's edge, `data-treatment="natural|mono|duotone|wash"` is its own treatment. It fits the media slots of the hero, split, features and posts sections and an article's cover and text. Tokens `--fd-site-ratio-*`.

The image ground, `data-ground="image"`: a picture behind the text, in `fd-site-backdrop` holding an `img` (`data-position="start|center|end"`). `data-scrim="dark|light"` is the scheme the text is set in, dark by default, and the text keeps 4.5:1 whatever the picture. It is built for the hero and the call to action band. `data-treatment` on the section treats its picture. Treatments are CSS filters and blend layers, no script. The `imagery` dial sets one for every picture that names none: `data-imagery="natural|mono|duotone|wash"` on `<html>` (natural is the default and writes no attribute), `dials.imagery` in the theme record, and a Pictures row in Tweaks. Tokens `--fd-site-image-*`, `--fd-site-mono-*`, `--fd-site-duo-*`, `--fd-site-wash*`.

`fd-site-pricing` tracks: plans and rows share their rows and columns, so a plan's parts line up across plans. Four plans sit four across, then two by two, then one column.

`fd-site-hero` bleed: `data-bleed` on a hero with `data-layout="stack"` runs the frame past the section's bottom, its cut edge fading into the ground. Without `data-bleed` the stack hero does not bleed.

**Older parts**

`fd-agent`, the layout of an agent screen: `fd-agent-side` (the sessions), `fd-agent-main`, `fd-agent-scroll` (the thread), `fd-agent-top` with `fd-agent-top-hide`, `fd-agent-dock` (the composer) with `fd-agent-dock-note`. `data-fill` takes the height under the top bar.

`fd-data-card`, a figure with its context and a toggle that opens more: `fd-data-card-head`, `fd-data-card-title`, `fd-data-card-desc`, `fd-data-card-figure`, `fd-data-card-unit`, `fd-data-card-toggle`, `fd-data-card-body`, `fd-data-card-more`; `data-state="closed"`; `fd:open`, `fd:close`.

`fd-listbox`, what a combobox opens: `fd-options` (`data-orientation="horizontal"`), `fd-option` with `fd-option-check`, `fd-option-main`, `fd-option-label`, `fd-option-desc`, `fd-option-meta`, and `fd-listbox-label`, `fd-listbox-empty`. `data-active` marks the row, `data-side="top"`, `data-fallback` marks the absolute-panel fallback.

`fd-matrix`, a switch for each category and channel: `data-cols="1|2|3|4"`; `fd-matrix-head`, `fd-matrix-row`, `fd-matrix-label`, `fd-matrix-text`, `fd-matrix-desc`, `fd-matrix-cell`.

`fd-result`, what an agent returned: `fd-result-head`, `fd-result-title`, `fd-result-count`, `fd-result-tools`, `fd-result-body`, `fd-result-table` (`data-mono`, `data-num`), `fd-result-foot`; `data-new` on a row that rises in.

`fd-use-agent`, a pill that offers the product to the person's own agent: `fd-use-agent-stack`, `fd-use-agent-tile` (`data-tone="info|neutral|violet"`).

`fd-viewswitch`, the same data in two views: `fd-viewswitch-item` buttons whose `data-value` names the panel with that `data-view`, inside `data-target` or the nearest `data-fd-views`; `data-size="md"`; `fd:change`.

Custom elements (not a family: each one is the root of a component above, and the catalogue maps it there): `fd-icon` (`icon`, `size`), `fd-chart` (`type`, `.data`), `fd-code` (`language`, `.code`), `fd-texture` (`pattern`, `marks`, …). Each element is its component's root and carries the root class (`fd-icon`, `fd-chart`, `fd-codeblock`, `fd-ascii`); use them in framework code, and the markup form on plain or server-rendered pages. The full list of attributes, properties and events is `contract/custom-elements.json`.
- **Looks** (not classes): a look is a named, complete theme record (`steady`, `operator`, `folio`, `hearth`, `instrument`, `marquee`, `gentle`, `clear`; `contract/looks/named`, `LOOKS` in `fire/js/looks.js`). `?look=<name>` applies one before first paint and sets `data-fd-look` on `<html>`; Tweaks' Looks row applies one whole.
