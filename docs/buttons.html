Components

Buttons

Soft by default, lit when they matter. A view has one primary button; everything else is a quiet fill that only darkens when you reach for it.

Variants

Secondary is the default and carries no border: a 4% ink wash that steps to 6% on hover and 8% when pressed. Primary is the only button with depth.

  • One primary per view. If two actions compete, the second is secondary or outline.
  • Use outline on a filled or busy surface where a wash would disappear; ghost for dismissals and toolbar actions.
  • inverse is for the single upsell or confirm on a quiet panel. Never beside a primary.

Sizes

36px is the default everywhere. On touch devices every size grows to a 40px or larger target without any markup change.

Icons

Icons sit at 72% of the label colour and reach full strength on hover. A trailing arrow marked data-nudge moves two pixels in its direction.

States

Pressing scales the button to 98%. Loading keeps the button's width and swaps its content for a spinner. Held state (an open menu, a toggle) uses the pressed fill.

Groups, text buttons and the floating action

Join related actions; use a text button when an action belongs inside a sentence or a card footer. A copy button swaps its label in place and a stack of tiles fans out on hover.

Rules

Do

Label with a verb and its object: “Start scraping”, “Copy referral link”, “Send invite”.

Don't

Stack two primaries, or use the accent for a destructive action. Delete is always the danger variant behind a confirmation.
