Cards & feedback

Objects, labels and messages. A card is for something you can name and open: a provider, a plan, a key. Everything else on a page is a hairline, a badge or a quiet line of status.

Card

White surface, a 1px faint edge and no shadow at rest. Head, body and foot are optional parts; the foot is separated by a hairline and sticks to the bottom when cards share a row.

How are credits counted?
A scrape costs 1 credit. Crawls cost 1 credit per page, and /extract adds 4 credits per page for structured output.
Do unused credits roll over?
Monthly credits reset on your renewal date. Credits from add-on packs never expire.
Can I scrape pages behind a login?
Yes. Pass cookies or a saved session in the request, or use /agent to sign in on your behalf.
