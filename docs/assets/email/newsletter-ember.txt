Ember Dispatch, November 2026

What shipped in Ember this month, what we measured across the pages our monitors watch, and one customer story. Four minutes to read.

SHIPPED
=======

Release: Monitors are generally available
Point a monitor at any URL, choose what counts as a change, and get a signed webhook with the diff. Checks run every 5 minutes to every 7 days, at 1 credit a check.
Read the release notes: https://ember.example/changelog#v2-14-0

Release: Typed JSON in the Python and Node SDKs
Pass a Pydantic model or a Zod schema to formats and get a typed object back, or a reason why a field is missing.
See the SDK changes: https://ember.example/changelog#v2-13-2

MEASURED
========

Report: 61% of pricing pages changed in 18 months
We watched 4,120 public pricing pages every 6 hours from May 2025 to October 2026. 2,513 of them changed at least once; 38% of all changes raised a price.
Read the pricing page index: https://ember.example/resources/pricing-page-index-2026

FROM CUSTOMERS
==============

Customer story: Fourteen scrapers became three API calls
Quarry Labs moved its price indexes to Ember in six weeks. Crawl spend fell from $19,100 in July to $12,600 in October.
Read the Quarry Labs story: https://ember.example/customers/quarry-labs
