Monitors are generally available

Point a monitor at any URL, choose what counts as a change, and get a signed webhook with the diff. Checks run every 5 minutes to every 7 days, at 1 credit a check.

Typed JSON in the Python and Node SDKs

Report

61% of pricing pages changed in 18 months

We watched 4,120 public pricing pages every 6 hours from May 2025 to October 2026. 2,513 of them changed at least once; 38% of all changes raised a price.
